Our Ingredients

Locally sourced. Carefully selected. Proudly Ugandan.

SOURCED WITH PRIDE

The Taste of
Uganda

Every bottle of YUTI BREWERIIES product tells the story of Uganda's rich agricultural heritage.

We are committed to sourcing the majority of our ingredients locally — supporting Ugandan farmers, strengthening local supply chains, and putting authentic Ugandan taste at the heart of everything we brew and distil.

From golden barley to botanicals and beyond, our ingredients are carefully selected to ensure consistent quality in every sip.

80% of Torrero's ingredients are locally sourced in Uganda
100% of Yura Beer ingredients are locally procured
