THE BEGINNING

A Company Built
by the People

YUTI BREWERIIES LTD is a brewing and distilling company established in 2018 with a bold vision — to put Uganda on the map as a premier producer of world-class beverages.

We boast of a state-of-the-art factory plant located at the Namanve Industrial Plant with a capacity of 800,000 Hectolitres of beer in production, making YUTI BREWERIIES a key player among the leading manufacturers of locally produced beers in Uganda with a market share of 50% of combined sales.

The company was officially launched by His Excellency Yoweri Kaguta Museveni on 4th July 2020 and has since been recognised by the Uganda Investment Authority, Uganda National Bureau of Standards and Uganda Manufacturers Association.

RESPONSIBILITY TO SOCIETY

Protecting Uganda's
Natural Heritage

We are passionate about creating a positive impact in the world that we live in.

On 19th November 2022, under our flagship beverage brand Yura Beer, we signed an agreement with Uganda Wildlife Education Centre (UWEC) to provide sanctuary for the White Tiger, aptly named "Yura" — the only of its kind in East Africa.

We committed UGX 30 million towards its welfare and surroundings, and pledged ongoing support for activities promoting public awareness about protecting endangered species.

Our ambition is to lead the charge for the conservation of Uganda's natural resources and biodiversity in order to create a socially and environmentally sustainable ecosystem for generations to come.
