WHO WE ARE

A World-Class
Manufacturer

YUTI BREWERIIES LTD is Uganda's fastest-growing brewing and distilling company, established in 2018 at the Namanve Industrial Plant.

Our state-of-the-art facility holds a production capacity of 800,000 Hectolitres of beer, making us a key player among leading manufacturers of locally produced beverages in Uganda — with a 50% market share of combined sales.

Officially launched by H.E. Yoweri Kaguta Museveni on 4th July 2020, we are recognised by the Uganda Investment Authority, Uganda National Bureau of Standards, and Uganda Manufacturers Association.
